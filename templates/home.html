FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding accounts, Python ATS scoring, and corporate verification.

What is PlaceSync?
PlaceSync is a modern Training and Placement Cell (TPO) management platform that connects educational institutions, graduating students, and corporate recruiters to streamline campus placement drives, ATS resumes, and scheduling.
How are student accounts initialized?
Colleges import student datasets via Excel/CSV spreadsheets. Once imported, students receive instant access credentials tied to their university enrollment numbers, with a forced password update prompt on first login.
How does the Python ATS Resume Score work?
Our integrated Python ATS parsing engine extracts text from PDF resumes, cross-referencing it against industry hiring standards to score formatting, section completeness, and keyword density.
How do placement drive eligibility filters work?
Colleges specify minimum CGPA thresholds, active backlog limits, and eligible engineering departments. Only candidates meeting all criteria can apply directly from their portal.
Is the platform secure and verified?
Yes. PlaceSync uses strict role-validated permissions. Recruiter company credentials are audited by college admins before placement drives go live.
