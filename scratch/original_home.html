Execution Workflow

Visualizing the Placement Journey

Follow the streamlined lifecycle of a placement drive as coordinates and events transition dynamically between panels.

Step 01

College Registers

The university administration establishes college profiles, configuring departments and grading ranges.

Step 02

Student Accounts Initialized

Admin imports student data via Excel/CSV sheets, auto-creating authenticated student portal entries.

Step 03

Students Build Profiles

Candidates log in to complete academic histories, edit programming skill categories, and upload resumes.

Step 04

Administrative Audit

College TPO Cell reviews partner companies, updating trust status and verifying credibility scores.

Step 05

Placement Drives Posted

Placement drives are published in the portal with CGPA, Department, and Skill eligibility requirements.

Step 06

Candidates Apply

Eligible students receive automated notifications, review detail cards, apply, and sync dates to calendars.

Step 07

Interviews and Screening

Candidates are shortlisted, interviews coordinated, and application stages tracked live.

Step 08

Final Offer Release

Offers are published, student statuses transition, analytics update, and career passports compile.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about accounts, resumes, and corporate recruiting.

What is PlaceSync?
PlaceSync is a centralized Training and Placement Cell management system designed to connect educational institutions, graduating students, and hiring companies to streamline campus recruitment drives, resumes, and scheduling.
Who is the platform for?
PlaceSync features dedicated portals for College Placement Officers (to manage drives, departments, and metrics) and candidates/students (to apply and build their portfolio).
How are students added?
Colleges can import student records directly from Excel/CSV spreadsheets. Once imported, students instantly receive login credentials via their enrollment numbers, prompting password updates on their first login.
How do placement drives work?
College admins establish drive details with specific criteria limits. Eligible students apply directly via their portals. Admins can then track application stages, download reports, and filter profiles.
Can students upload resumes?
Yes, students can upload PDF resumes, download or delete versions, and check text parsing.
How is the ATS Score generated?
Our integrated python parsing algorithms read the text inside the uploaded resume, matching it against industry standards, checking section completions, and highlighting missing keywords.
Is the platform secure?
Absolutely. All users log in via role-validated accounts. Student profiles and academic records are visible only to verified colleges.
